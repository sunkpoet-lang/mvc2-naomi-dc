## ForkAde's additions

`ports/port/forkade/` holds what ForkAde adds to the port (save states, the menu bar, replays, online play). Some of
it is copied from other GPL-3.0 projects (recomp-common, ForkAde's CPS and Street Fighter III forks), which the
AGPL-3.0 allows to be combined with this repository's code; `ports/port/forkade/README.md` lists each part, where it
comes from and its license. GekkoNet (BSD-2-Clause) is fetched by the build, not kept here.
